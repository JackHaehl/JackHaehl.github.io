Dementia AR

What dementia does to vision → a world caregivers can step into and try to parse.

The impossible ask

Replicate Oakwood Creative Care's analogue empathy training kit (distorting glasses, gloves, and headphones playing disorienting sounds while trainees attempt everyday tasks) in AR, so empathy training for caregivers could be cheaper and more scalable than the manual kit. The project was formally for Forsche Technologies, as Oakwood Creative Care's interface with ASU, and served Oakwood, a dementia care centre working under a Meta grant.

What was invisible

What dementia actually does to vision. It is not ordinary colourblindness: optic nerve decay collapses colour discrimination, so colours mush together and the world becomes harder to parse.

What I built

Quest 3 passthrough → K-means colour lookup table applied live → collapsed-colour view, plus tunnel vision and disorienting audio

A Quest 3 passthrough simulation of dementia visual symptoms. My signature build was an adjustable colour lookup table (LUT) applied live to the passthrough feed, built with K-means clustering to collapse similar colours together and simulate that colour discrimination loss. The build also included tunnel vision and other medically based visual symptom simulations, and disorienting audio. The other symptom effects are not named here, because their exact condition names have not been verified.

Meta Quest 3 passthrough, Unity / C#

What it made possible

Recognition, stated honestly. The project won Best Project at the ASU Senior CS Capstone Showcase. Distribution was limited: the app lives on a closed test channel only, a brief post-capstone freelance continuation fizzled, and its current use is unknown. No public release is claimed, and no training use is claimed.
