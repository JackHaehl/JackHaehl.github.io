A thought in your head → an object in your hand, without leaving the headset.

The impossible ask

Build a creation platform where a spoken idea becomes a physical object, without the user ever leaving the headset.

What was invisible

An idea that existed only as words: no model, no mesh, nothing a printer, or a team, could act on.

What I built

Speak → LLM re-prompt → generated image (spatially editable) → image-to-3D model you can grab → cleaned mesh → Bambu printer

The complete spatial creation flow end to end, as sole immersive-tech lead and primary Unity developer. Speak a request, an LLM expands it to an image-ready prompt, the image is generated and spatially editable (draw or circle a region to change it), place the image on a pedestal, image-to-3D in-headset, grab the model, send a cleaned mesh to a connected printer. Plus a real-world capture variant: photograph a wall or space, generate an object in context.

Unity / C#, Meta Quest 3 MR (passthrough), Photon, Python services, LLM re-prompting, text-to-image, image-to-3D APIs, Bambu printer handoff

What it made possible

Delivery and decision enabled. Never publicly released. Demoed live in real time at public events (Tempe Geek Night, Women of Web3, about 80 people across both; kids designed toys in-headset and collected the print about 2 hours later) and on facility tours to a couple dozen partners, investors, and universities. Output was toys, demo objects, and pre-CAD visualisation models, never a functional installed part. I pushed to re-aim it from an undefined public audience to internal use; that pushback is part of the story, told plainly.
