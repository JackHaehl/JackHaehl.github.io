About

I'm a creative technologist in Glendale, Arizona. For six years, across university research labs and a startup, the job has been the same: take an idea that sounds impossible, visualise a magnetic field, program a robot by posing it in mid-air, turn a spoken sentence into a printed object, and make it real enough to touch, usually within a week.

Most recently I was XR Engineering Director at Startup Central in Tempe, the sole immersive-tech lead in a flat 5 to 12 person team, building a voice-to-3D-print creation platform on Quest 3 and programming a dual-arm robot bartender that now operates in a client's home. Before that I led VR development at ASU's Digital Discovery Initiative, hiring and managing seven student developers, where our lunar mission-planning tools were shown by our partner Lunar Outpost to NASA, and the pipelines I built are still in use. My work has been co-authored at AGU 2024 and CHI '22, and it started, in 2020, with a handheld device that lets students see magnetic fields.

I'm looking for the next impossible brief: 0-to-1 R&D, innovation-lab, and creative-technology work, in the Phoenix metro, remote across the US, or somewhere worth relocating for.
